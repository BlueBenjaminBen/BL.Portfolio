About

I'm a junior majoring in Computer Science with a concentration in Cybersecurity at the University of Delaware. I have always been facinated with technology at a young age and was always toying around on my dad's computer; figuring out the use of different tools, settings, commands, etc. I eventually found the internet and foolishly downloaded a Trojan virus from an AD promising the free download of Minecraft.


This ignited my curiosity of the security and services that go into the devices we use everyday to protect us from such threats. I quickly became the "IT guy" of my family--troubleshooting printer network issues, setting up devices, and eventually monitoring home network traffic. What started as a hobby evolved into a deep passion. Now I want to share this passion beyond just my family by spreading awareness and aiding others navigate and secure their technology.

I invite you to check out my work, projects, and experiences that will give you a better insight into my skillset, what I'm passionate in, and who I am!
